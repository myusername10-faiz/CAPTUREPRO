Privacy, Permissions & About

Android Permissions Policy

CapturePro requests permissions only when needed:
• MediaProjection Consent: Fresh system dialog per capture session (Android 14+ supports Single App or Entire Display).
• RECORD_AUDIO: Only when Microphone recording is enabled.
• CAMERA: Only when Facecam is enabled.
• SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Optional for movable floating controls.
• Foreground Service: Keeps recording status visible with a persistent Stop button.

Meeting Etiquette & Consent: Always obtain appropriate permission from meeting participants before recording calls or conversations. CapturePro never bypasses protected content or app audio restrictions.
